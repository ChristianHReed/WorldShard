# WorldShard — Map & World Design (v0.5)

## Origin
Thor's hammer fell and broke the Earth apart, sending its broken pieces into space, where they collided together to make new worlds. This world is one of those.

## World structure (center → edge)
1. **The Island** – the landmass, split into 6 regions. (Concept sketch label: "the island of 6 Realms." The term used in design is "regions.")
2. **The Rim Sea** – the island sits in a half-sphere bowl of water, held in place by gravity modified by magic.
3. **The Cloudwall** – a ring of clouds around the circumference. It is the spawn point for all of the island's weather. Players can reach it in the late game only.

### Weather from the Cloudwall
- Weather spawns at points along the Cloudwall and drifts inward across the sea toward the island.
- The region facing a storm gets hit first, so each region has a characteristic weather pattern based on what part of the wall it faces.

## Island geography (from concept sketch)
- **Central peaks:** Mount Everest and Mount St. Helens are the two peaks in the middle of the island. The regions sit at their combined base.
- **Underside:** the rocky underside of the broken chunk hangs below the surface.
- **Interior lines:** mark both region borders and ridgelines/terrain.
- **Right edge:** spiky points are cliffs.
- **Lower-right inlet:** a curved inlet that is a sea.

## Day & night
The world spins around the sun.

## Scale & travel
- 3–5 days to cross one region.
- Two weeks to sail from shore to the clouds.

## Regions
6 regions arranged around the central peaks, each with its own native inhabitants, nature and geography. Tone is mixed by region. Working names are compass directions until renamed.

### North
Rock mines.

### Northeast
Lava flows and desert.

### Southeast
Mid forest with fields. Contains the lower-right inlet sea.

### South
Dark forest.

### Southwest
Grasslands.

### Northwest
Lakes and forest, more swampy.
